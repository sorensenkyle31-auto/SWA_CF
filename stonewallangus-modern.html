Our Story

Three Decades of Family Farming

Jim Matheny and his family have been raising Black Angus cattle at 17719 Spielman Road since 1989. Every animal is born and bred right here — fed grass and grain we grow ourselves — and sold directly to our community with pride and honesty.

🌾

Farm Raised

All cattle born and raised on our property. Never outsourced, never factory-farmed.

🚫

Hormone Free

Zero growth hormones. Antibiotics only in life-or-death situations.

🏆

Award Winning

Supreme Grand Champion and multiple class-winning cattle.

🤝

Direct to You

Buy by the piece, quarter, or half — straight from us to your freezer.
