Privacy Policy & SMS Terms

Last updated: August 2026

Stone Wall Angus ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it when you place an order, book an appointment, or otherwise interact with our website at this domain.

What We Collect

When you place an order or book an appointment, we collect your name, email address, phone number, pickup/appointment preferences, and any notes you provide. We use this solely to fulfill your order or appointment and to communicate with you about it.

SMS / Text Messaging Terms

If you check the box to receive text messages, you agree to receive order and appointment status updates (e.g. invoice ready, order ready for pickup, appointment confirmation) from Stone Wall Angus at the phone number you provide.

  • Consent is optional. You do not have to agree to receive texts to place an order or book an appointment — you'll still receive updates by email either way.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on your order/appointment activity.
  • Opt out anytime by replying STOP to any text we send, or by contacting us at stonewallangus1@myactv.net or (240) 818-8317.
  • Reply HELP for help at any time.
  • We do not sell or share your phone number with third parties for marketing purposes.

How We Use Your Information

We use your contact information only to process and fulfill your order or appointment, send order/appointment status updates, and respond to questions you send us. We do not sell your personal information.

Data Retention

We retain order and appointment records as needed for our business and legal recordkeeping purposes.
